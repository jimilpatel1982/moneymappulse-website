Mortgage Calculator

Estimate mortgage payments, interest and loan-to-value with the Mortgage Calculator.

What this tool does

Estimates monthly mortgage payments, interest-only payments and loan-to-value (LTV) based on property value, deposit, rate and term.

Why it is useful

Before you buy or remortgage, you can test whether a payment fits your household budget and how deposit size affects borrowing.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open the Mortgage Calculator from the hub.
  2. Enter property value and deposit.
  3. Enter interest rate and mortgage term in years.
  4. Review repayment and interest-only results.

Input fields explained

Property valuePrice of the home you are buying or current value for remortgage planning.
DepositCash you put down. Mortgage amount is usually value minus deposit.
Mortgage amountAmount borrowed (often calculated automatically).
Interest rateAnnual rate for illustration — use a rate you expect, not a guaranteed offer.
Mortgage termLength of the loan in years (for example 25 or 30).

Worked example

Property value: £350,000

Deposit: £70,000

Mortgage amount: £280,000

Interest rate: 4.5%

Term: 25 years

Monthly payment (repayment): illustrative figure shown in app

LTV: 80%

Understanding your results

Monthly payment is your estimated repayment mortgage cost. Interest only shows payments if you paid interest alone (not suitable for all products). LTV ratio helps you see how much equity you have — lower LTV often means better rates in real lending, but this app only estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a mortgage offer?

No. Figures are estimates for planning, not lending advice.

Does it include fees or insurance?

Only what you enter. Add stamp duty, fees and insurance separately in your budget.
