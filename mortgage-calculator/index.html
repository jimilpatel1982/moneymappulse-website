Mortgage Calculator

Estimate mortgage payments, interest and loan-to-value with the Mortgage Calculator.

What this tool does

Estimates repayment and interest-only payments plus loan-to-value (LTV) from property value, deposit, rate and term.

Understanding your mortgage results

Understanding inputs

Property valuePurchase price or current value.
DepositCash you contribute upfront.
Interest rateAnnual rate for illustration.
Mortgage termLoan length in years.

Understanding results

Compare the monthly payment with your House Budget. LTV helps you understand equity — lower LTV often attracts better rates in real lending, but this tool only estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a mortgage offer?

No. Figures are for planning only, not lending advice.

Does it include fees?

Add stamp duty, legal fees and insurance separately.

Can I use estimated property values?

Yes for early planning.

What is a good LTV?

Below 80% is common for better rates, but products vary.

How often should I recalculate?

When rates, property value or deposit plans change.
