Frequently asked questions

Is this investment advice?

No. It is an educational planning aid.

Which risk profile should I pick?

Choose one that matches how much short-term fluctuation you could tolerate.

Can I change profile later?

Yes. Update and refresh your snapshot.

Why adjust for inflation?

Retirement spending in 20 years will not buy the same as today.

How often should I review?

When your goals or comfort with risk changes.
