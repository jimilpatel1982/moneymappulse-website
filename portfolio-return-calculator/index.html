Portfolio Return Calculator

Estimate inflation-adjusted returns and choose a risk profile for planning.

What this tool does

Helps you explore expected portfolio returns based on asset mix or risk level, often with inflation in mind, so planning assumptions are more realistic.

Why it is useful

Retirement and investment calculators need a return assumption. This tool supports choosing a profile that matches how much risk you are comfortable taking on paper.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Portfolio Return Calculator.
  2. Review asset categories or risk profiles available in the app.
  3. Enter allocations or select a profile (for example Cautious, Balanced, Growth).
  4. Save results to feed your Financial Health Snapshot risk profile.

Input fields explained

Asset allocationHow your money is split across types such as cash, bonds and shares.
Expected returnIllustrative long-term return for each asset class or combined portfolio.
InflationOptional adjustment so you see real (inflation-adjusted) returns.
Risk profileLabel summarising your mix (feeds the dashboard card).

Understanding your results

The summary explains expected volatility in plain terms. Your selected risk profile appears on the Financial Health Snapshot so you can align retirement assumptions with how you invest on paper.
