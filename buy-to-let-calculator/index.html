Frequently asked questions

Does this include tax?

Rental tax is not advice here — plan separately with a qualified professional.

Can I use estimated rent?

Yes. Use local letting agent guides or conservative figures.

What expenses should I include?

Insurance, maintenance, void periods, agent fees and mortgage interest.

Is yield or ROI more important?

Use both: yield compares rent to price; ROI compares profit to cash you put in.

How often should I update?

When purchase price, rent or mortgage assumptions change.
