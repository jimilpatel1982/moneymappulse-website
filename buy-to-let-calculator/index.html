Buy To Let Calculator

Evaluate rental property using cash flow, ROI and yield estimates.

What this tool does

Estimates rental income, costs, mortgage payments, net cash flow, ROI and gross yield for a buy-to-let property scenario.

Why it is useful

Compare property against other uses of your cash and see whether rent covers costs after mortgage and expenses.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Buy To Let Calculator.
  2. Enter purchase price, deposit and mortgage details.
  3. Enter expected rent and running costs.
  4. Review summary and save if you want to compare later.

Input fields explained

Purchase priceProperty purchase amount.
Deposit / cash investedYour equity in the deal.
Rental incomeExpected monthly rent.
ExpensesManagement, maintenance, insurance, voids allowance, etc.
Mortgage paymentMonthly cost if financed.

Worked example

Monthly net income: £362.50

Annual net income: £4,350

ROI: 4.44%

Gross yield: 6.00%

Understanding your results

Monthly net income is rent minus mortgage and expenses. ROI relates return to cash invested. Gross yield compares annual rent to property price before costs. Use all three together — not in isolation.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include tax?

Tax on rental income is not personal advice here; speak to a qualified professional for tax.

Is void period included?

Include voids in expenses if the app provides a field or add a conservative allowance.
