Features

A personal finance dashboard and planning toolkit to help you track progress and make informed decisions.

Financial Health Snapshot

Your dashboard brings together savings, net worth, risk profile and retirement readiness. Interactive summary cards help you see how budgeting, net worth and retirement tools connect in one place.

Retirement planning

Explore retirement projections, your retirement journey, checkpoints and funding breakdowns. See whether your current savings and assumptions put you on track for the lifestyle you plan for.

Net worth tracking

Record assets and liabilities to see your overall net worth. Version 2.0 improves how you review liquid and illiquid assets so you can understand what is accessible versus longer-term value.

Investment growth

Project future investment value using lump-sum and monthly contributions. Reference milestones help you explore how assumptions affect long-term growth — for planning, not guarantees.

Buy to let evaluation

Estimate monthly net income, ROI, gross yield, mortgage payments and expenses for rental property scenarios. Compare opportunities using cash flow and return metrics.

Household budgeting

Build a household budget across income and expense categories. Understand monthly savings and spending patterns as the foundation for your wider financial plan.

Calculator hub

Access budgeting, retirement, investment, mortgage, net worth, portfolio return and buy-to-let tools from one hub. Saved results feed into your Financial Health Snapshot where applicable.

Privacy-first design

No login. No cloud account. No backend. Data is stored locally on your device. You stay in control of the information you enter.
