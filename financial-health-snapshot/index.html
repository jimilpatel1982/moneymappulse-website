Financial Health Snapshot

Learn how the Financial Health Snapshot dashboard brings together savings, net worth, risk profile and retirement readiness.

What this tool does

The Financial Health Snapshot is your personal dashboard in MoneyMap Pulse. It summarises key numbers from your calculators so you can see how you are doing without opening every tool separately.

Why it is useful

Budgeting, net worth, investments and retirement can feel disconnected. The snapshot connects them in one view so you can spot gaps, confirm progress and decide what to work on next.

How to complete the calculator

You do not enter data directly on the snapshot screen. Complete the calculators below, save your results, then open your profile or home dashboard to refresh the snapshot.

  1. Run the House Budget Calculator and save your budget.
  2. Complete the Net Worth Calculator with assets and liabilities.
  3. Use the Portfolio Return Calculator to choose a risk profile.
  4. Run the Retirement Calculator and save your projection.
  5. Return to the Financial Health Snapshot to review summary cards.

What each summary card means

Monthly savingsLeftover income after expenses for the month. Pulled from your saved House Budget.
Net worthTotal assets minus total liabilities. Pulled from your saved Net Worth calculation.
Risk profileYour selected planning profile (for example Balanced). Pulled from the Portfolio Return Calculator.
Retirement statusWhether your projected fund meets your required fund. Pulled from the Retirement Calculator.

Where dashboard data comes from

  • Monthly savings — House Budget Calculator
  • Net worth — Net Worth Calculator
  • Risk profile — Portfolio Return Calculator
  • Retirement — Retirement Calculator

Understanding your results

On track means your projected retirement fund meets or exceeds the fund you need based on your assumptions. Shortfall means the projection falls below what is required — you may want to adjust savings, retirement age or spending assumptions in the Retirement Calculator.

Tap any summary card to open the related detail view (budget breakdown, net worth list, risk profile notes or retirement projection).

Frequently asked questions

Why is a card empty?

The snapshot shows saved calculator results. Open the linked calculator, enter your figures and save.

Do I need an account?

No. Data stays on your device. Saving within the app links calculators to your snapshot.

How often should I update?

Update whenever your income, expenses, assets or retirement assumptions change.
