House Budget Calculator

Track household income, expenses and monthly savings with the House Budget Calculator.

What this tool does

The House Budget Calculator helps you record income and spending by category, then shows how much you save each month and your savings rate.

Why it is useful

Knowing your monthly savings is the foundation for retirement planning, investments and your Financial Health Snapshot. It turns everyday spending into a clear cash-flow picture.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Financial Calculators and choose House Budget.
  2. Enter all income sources for the household.
  3. Enter expenses in each category that applies to you.
  4. Review totals and save your budget.

Input fields explained

IncomeMoney coming in each month (salary, self-employment, benefits, rent received, etc.).
ExpensesMoney going out, grouped into categories such as housing, transport, food and leisure.
SavingsIncome minus expenses — what is left at the end of the month.
Savings rateSavings divided by income, shown as a percentage.

Worked example

Income: £4,700

Expenses: £3,221

Savings: £1,479

Savings rate: 31.5%

Understanding your results

The summary shows whether your plan is sustainable and feeds Monthly savings on your Financial Health Snapshot. Use category totals to see where you might adjust spending.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use net or gross income?

Use the amount that actually lands in your account if that is how you plan day to day.

Can I budget monthly if I am paid weekly?

Yes. Enter typical monthly totals or average your weekly figures across a month.
