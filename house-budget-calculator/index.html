Frequently asked questions

What is a good savings rate?

Many planners suggest 10–20% as a starting goal, but your target depends on your goals and costs.

Should I use net or gross income?

Use the amount you actually receive if that is how you manage cash flow.

Can I use estimated values?

Yes. Refine categories when you have exact figures.

How often should I update my budget?

Monthly is ideal; at minimum after income or major expense changes.

What if my expenses exceed income?

The calculator will show a negative balance — a signal to review spending or income.
