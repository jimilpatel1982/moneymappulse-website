Investment Calculator

Project future investment value from lump sums and monthly contributions.

What this tool does

Projects how an investment might grow using a starting amount, monthly contributions, expected return and time horizon.

Understanding your investment projection

Select a numbered marker on the screenshot to explore each part.

①

Initial investment

Amount invested today.

Example: £10,000

Understanding inputs

Initial investmentLump sum at the start.
Monthly contributionsRegular additions.
Expected returnAnnual growth assumption.
Time horizonYears you stay invested.

Understanding results

Future value is the projected total. Investment growth is future value minus what you contributed. Milestone years help you see progress over time.

Worked example

Initial: £10,000

Monthly: £500

Return: 6% per year

Horizon: 16 years

Future value: £583,242 (illustrative)

Frequently asked questions

Will I achieve this return?

No projection is guaranteed. Markets go up and down.

Can I use estimated values?

Yes — try conservative and optimistic scenarios.

Does this include tax?

Treat results as simplified unless you model tax separately.

What return should I assume?

Many beginners use a modest long-term figure; the Portfolio Return Calculator can help.

How often should I update?

Yearly or when your contribution plan changes.
