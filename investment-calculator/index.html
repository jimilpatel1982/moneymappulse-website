Investment Calculator

Project future investment value from lump sums and monthly contributions.

What this tool does

Projects how savings might grow over time using an initial amount, regular contributions, expected return and time horizon.

Why it is useful

Helps you set realistic expectations for long-term goals and compare different contribution levels — without promising actual market performance.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Investment Calculator.
  2. Enter initial investment and monthly contribution.
  3. Set expected annual return and investment period.
  4. Choose contribution timing if shown (start or end of month).
  5. Review future value and milestone projections.

Input fields explained

Initial investmentLump sum already invested or starting balance.
Monthly contributionsAmount you plan to add each month.
Expected returnAnnual growth rate assumption — not a guarantee.
Time horizonNumber of years you plan to stay invested.

Worked example

Initial investment: £10,000

Monthly contributions: £500

Expected return: 6% per year

Time horizon: 20 years

Future value: shown in app (illustrative)

Total contributions: £130,000

Investment growth: future value minus contributions

Understanding your results

Future value is the projected balance at the end of the period. Total contributions is what you paid in. Investment growth is the projected gain. Reference milestones (5, 10, 15 years) help you see progress along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Will I really get this return?

No projection is guaranteed. Markets vary; use conservative assumptions.

Does this include tax?

Unless you add tax assumptions elsewhere, treat results as before-tax illustrations.
