User guide

Net Worth Calculator

Calculate assets, liabilities and track your net worth over time.

What this tool does

Lists what you own (assets) and what you owe (liabilities), then calculates net worth.

Why it is useful

Net worth is a single number for your overall position — useful for goals, retirement planning and the Financial Health Snapshot.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Net Worth Calculator.
  2. Add assets (property, savings, investments, pensions, etc.).
  3. Add liabilities (mortgages, loans, credit cards).
  4. Review totals and save.

Input fields explained

AssetsItems with positive value you own.
LiabilitiesDebts you still owe.
Net worthAssets minus liabilities.
Liquid vs illiquidWhere shown, separates easy-to-access cash from property and long-term holdings.

Worked example

Assets: £550,000

Liabilities: £250,000

Net worth: £300,000

Understanding your results

Totals feed the Net worth card on your snapshot. Update when you buy property, pay down debt or build savings.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include my home?

Include property if you want a full picture; some people track with and without home equity separately.

How often should I update?

Every few months or after major life changes.
