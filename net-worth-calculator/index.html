Frequently asked questions

What should I include as assets?

Include major items: home equity, savings, investments and pensions. Skip small household goods unless material.

Should I include my home?

Include it for a full picture, or track a version without property if you prefer.

Can I use estimated values?

Yes. Update when you have formal valuations.

How often should I update?

Every few months or after buying property, paying off debt or large investments.

What is wealth percentile?

A rough comparison for context — not a ranking you need to optimise.
