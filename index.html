How to use MoneyMap Pulse

Follow these simple steps to build your financial picture.

  1. Create your budget

    Use the House Budget Calculator to track income, expenses and monthly savings.

    Example: Income £4,700 · Expenses £3,221 · Savings £1,479

  2. Calculate your net worth

    Add your assets and liabilities to understand your current financial position.

    Example: Assets £550,000 · Liabilities £250,000 · Net worth £300,000

  3. Plan your retirement

    Use your savings and investment assumptions to see whether you are on track for retirement.

    Example: Projected pension fund £1,092,666 · Required fund £1,085,649 · Status: On track

  4. Explore investments

    Use Investment and Buy To Let calculators to compare opportunities and understand future growth.

  5. Monitor everything from the Financial Health Dashboard

    Track your financial progress from one place.
