Frequently asked questions

What should count as retirement income?

Include workplace pensions, private pensions, investments and other sources you expect.

Does this include State Pension?

Only if you add it in your inputs.

Can I use estimated values?

Yes — refine as you get pension statements.

What if I have a shortfall?

Try higher savings, later retirement or lower spending assumptions in the calculator.

How often should I update?

At least yearly, or after a pay rise, bonus or major life change.
