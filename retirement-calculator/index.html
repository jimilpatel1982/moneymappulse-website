User guide

Retirement Calculator

Review retirement projections, journey checkpoints and whether you are on track.

What this tool does

Projects retirement fund growth, required fund at retirement, monthly income needs and journey checkpoints from today through retirement age.

Why it is useful

Shows whether current savings habits might support your target lifestyle and highlights shortfalls early enough to adjust contributions or retirement age on paper.

How to complete the calculator

  1. Open Retirement Calculator.
  2. Enter current age, retirement age and life expectancy if asked.
  3. Add current savings, monthly contributions and return assumptions.
  4. Enter expected retirement spending.
  5. Save and review journey and funding breakdown.

Input fields explained

Current savingsPensions and investments counted toward retirement.
Monthly savingRegular contributions until retirement.
Retirement ageAge you plan to stop full-time work.
Retirement spendingMonthly income you want in retirement (today's money or as labelled in app).
Return assumptionGrowth rate applied to projections.

Understanding your results

On track means projected assets meet required fund. Shortfall means you may need higher savings, later retirement or lower spending assumptions. The retirement journey timeline shows checkpoints at today, retirement and later ages with funding breakdowns.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include State Pension?

Only if you enter it. Add state and workplace pensions explicitly.

Are tax rules modelled?

The app provides estimates; tax treatment may be simplified.
